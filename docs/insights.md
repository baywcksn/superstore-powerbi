# Business Insights

## Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Analytics dashboard provides an overview of sales performance, profitability, order volume, and category performance based on the Superstore dataset.

The analysis focuses on identifying sales patterns and understanding which product categories contribute to overall business performance.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2.30M |
| Total Profit | $286.40K |
| Total Orders | 5K |

## Sales Performance by Category

The three product categories generated the following sales:

| Category | Sales |
|---|---:|
| Technology | $836,154.03 |
| Furniture | $741,999.80 |
| Office Supplies | $719,047.03 |

Technology generated the highest sales among the three categories, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

## Profitability

Total profit reached approximately **$286.40K** across the dataset.

Sales and profit are analyzed separately in the dashboard because a category with high sales does not necessarily generate the highest profit.

The **Profit by Category** visualization provides a separate view to identify differences between sales performance and profitability.

## Sales Trend

The **Monthly Sales Trend** visualization shows how sales changed over time from 2014 to 2017.

The time-series analysis can be used to identify periods of higher and lower sales activity and provides a foundation for further analysis such as year-over-year and month-over-month comparisons.

## Interactive Analysis

The dashboard includes two filters:

- **Region**
- **Category**

These filters allow users to analyze sales, profit, orders, and category performance within a specific segment of the dataset.

For example, selecting a specific region changes the KPI values and visualizations according to the selected filter context.

## Key Takeaways

1. The dataset generated approximately **$2.30M in total sales**.
2. Total profit reached approximately **$286.40K**.
3. **Technology** recorded the highest sales among the three product categories.
4. Sales performance and profitability should be evaluated separately because higher sales do not necessarily indicate higher profit.
5. Regional and category filters allow users to explore differences in business performance.
